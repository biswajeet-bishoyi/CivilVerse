**Project:** CivilVerse

# Content Vision

CivilVerse is a knowledge platform, not a blog.

Every piece of content should educate, connect to related topics, and support AI-assisted learning through Archie.

---

# Editorial Principles

- Accuracy before speed
- Educational value first
- Cite reliable references
- Explain complex ideas visually
- Keep content evergreen where possible
- Regularly review and update

---

# Primary Content Types

## Engineering Concepts
Examples:
- Shear
- Bending
- Buckling
- Soil Consolidation

## Engineering Marvels
Dedicated pages for iconic structures.

## Materials Library

Concrete, Steel, Timber, FRP, Glass, etc.

## Standards Guides

Educational explanations of IS, Eurocode, ACI and related standards.

## Construction Methods

Step-by-step construction techniques with diagrams and animations.

## News

Infrastructure projects, technology, sustainability and industry updates.

## Case Studies

Successes, failures and engineering lessons learned.

Every content item includes:

- Title
- Summary
- Slug
- Tags
- Difficulty
- Reading time

- Revision history
