# Product Overview

CivilVerse is an AI-first civil engineering platform combining learning, engineering news, infrastructure exploration, interactive content, and an AI assistant named Archie.

## Objectives

- Become the leading civil engineering knowledge platform.
- Deliver trustworthy educational content.
- Provide AI-assisted learning and research.
- Build an interconnected engineering knowledge graph.

## Target Users

- Students
- Professors
- Structural Engineers
- Site Engineers
- Researchers
- Construction Professionals
- Enthusiasts

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

- Home page
- Search
- Engineering articles
- Engineering marvels
- News
- Archie AI assistant
- User accounts
- Bookmarks
- Engineering calculators
- Responsive UI

## Key Modules

### Engineering News
Latest infrastructure, technology, sustainability, and construction updates.

### Engineering Marvels
Dedicated pages covering history, design, construction, materials, structural systems, and multimedia.

### Learn
Structured learning paths with quizzes, diagrams, and AI tutoring.

### Archie
Educational AI capable of explaining concepts, summarizing PDFs, answering questions, and interpreting engineering images.

### Calculators
Beam, slab, concrete, steel weight, units, and quantity estimation tools.

## Success Metrics

- Monthly active users
- Session duration
- AI engagement
- Search success rate
- Returning users
- Completion of learning paths
